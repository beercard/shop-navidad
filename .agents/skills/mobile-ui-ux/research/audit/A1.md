## 0. Scope and result in one paragraph

About 95 specific claims were spot-checked (list in Appendix A). Roughly 75 matched the notes exactly (numbers, dates, percentages, sample sizes, version gates). The files are unusually faithful; evidence tags are mostly honest. The real problems are not typos. They are (a) 8 true mismatches or misattributions, (b) 9 cross-file contradictions where the skill inherited two conflicting note recommendations without reconciling them (tab counts, swipe-action counts, snackbar duration, feedback ladder order), (c) about 12 places where an opinion/snippet is written as a rule without an `[H]`/SNIPPET tag, (d) coverage holes against the brief (drag-and-drop, double-tap, breadcrumbs beyond mobile web, top navigation in the comparison table, product-patterns has no comparison tables).

Severity scale: HIGH = produces contradictory agent output or a false statement of fact; MED = wrong label, misattribution, or unreconciled number; LOW = precision or tag issue.
