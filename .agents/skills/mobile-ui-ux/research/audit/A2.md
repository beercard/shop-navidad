# Audit A2: forms.md, states-feedback.md, ux-writing.md, accessibility.md

Auditor: independent, read-only. No skill file was edited.
Ground truth: `research/notes/06`, `07`, `02` (plus `01`, `04`, `05`, `09`, `10`, `12` where a claim traces there).
I also re-checked four claims against live primary sources, because the notes were produced through a summarizing fetch tool: NIST SP 800-63B-4, the W3C text-size article, and the A List Apart inline-validation article. Those checks are marked "(live)".
Line refs: `F:` forms.md, `S:` states-feedback.md, `U:` ux-writing.md, `A:` accessibility.md. `N06/N07/N02` = notes.

Overall: fidelity to the notes is high. About 65 numeric and factual claims were checked and the large majority match. The real problems are (a) one NIST misstatement, (b) one unsupported study detail, (c) a misread of the IBM text-expansion table, (d) several cross-file contradictions in thresholds (select-control cut-offs, snackbar and undo duration, reduced-motion crossfade, loader delay), and (e) real coverage holes: unsaved-changes and leave-guard, save state, and the deleted-item empty state.
